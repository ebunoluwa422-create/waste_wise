# WasteWise — Frequently Asked Questions

---

## Getting Started

**What is WasteWise?**
WasteWise is a platform that lets you schedule waste pickups, track your collection in real time, and receive updates from the waste management team — all from one account.

**Is WasteWise free to use?**
Creating an account is completely free. You only pay when you schedule and confirm an actual pickup.

**Do I need to download an app?**
No — WasteWise runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to install. Just visit the site and log in.

**What areas does WasteWise currently serve?**
WasteWise is currently built for Ile-Ife neighborhoods.

---

## Account & Login

**How do I create an account?**
Click "Create your account" on the homepage, fill in your name, pickup address, email, and password, then click "Create account."

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Password reset isn't currently available in-app — this is a known limitation we're looking to address in a future update.

**Can I change my registered pickup address after signing up?**
You can update your address details from your Profile section on your dashboard.

**Why do I need to provide an address when signing up?**
Your address is how the collection team knows where to send a truck. Without it, a pickup can't be scheduled.

---

## Scheduling & Sorting

**How do I schedule a pickup?**
From your dashboard, go to "Schedule pickup," choose a date and time window, then confirm. Your pickup is scheduled immediately using the address on your account.

**Can I choose a specific time for my pickup?**
You choose from available time windows (e.g., 8:00–10:00 AM) rather than an exact minute — this allows the collection team to plan routes efficiently.

**What waste categories can I select?**
Organic, Plastic, Paper, Glass, Metal, and Hazardous.

**Why does the waste category matter?**
Selecting the right category helps the collection team come prepared with the right handling for your waste — especially important for hazardous materials.

**Can I schedule more than one pickup?**
Yes. Each pickup you schedule gets its own unique request ID, so you can track multiple pickups independently.

**Can I cancel or reschedule a pickup after confirming it?**
There's currently no way to cancel or reschedule a pickup once it's confirmed — this is a known limitation we're looking to address in a future update.

## Payment

**What payment methods are accepted?**
Card payments are currently supported through our secure payment provider.

**Is my payment information safe?**
Yes — payments are processed through a secure third-party payment provider. WasteWise does not store your card details.

**When am I charged?**
You're charged when you confirm a scheduled pickup, not before.

**Can I get a refund if my pickup doesn't happen?**
There's currently no self-service way to request a refund in-app — this is a known limitation we're looking to address in a future update.

---

## Tracking Your Pickup

**How do I know if my pickup is on its way?**
Your dashboard shows a live status tracker: Scheduled → En Route → Arrived → Collected. Check back anytime to see the current stage.

**Do I get notified when the truck arrives?**
Status updates are visible on your dashboard as they happen — no need to keep the app open constantly.

**What does "Scheduled" vs. "En Route" mean?**
"Scheduled" means your request is confirmed but collection hasn't started yet. "En Route" means the collection team is actively on the way to your address.

**My pickup status hasn't changed in a while — what should I do?**
Status updates depend on the admin team updating your pickup's progress. There's currently no in-app way to request an update directly.

---

## Messages & Notifications

**How will I know if the WasteWise team needs to reach me?**
Any message from the admin team appears directly on your dashboard, and a copy is also sent to your registered email.

**I didn't receive an email — what should I check?**
First check your spam or junk folder. Also confirm the email address on your account is correct. Email delivery can occasionally take a few minutes.

**Can I reply to a message from the admin team?**
No — messaging in WasteWise is currently one-directional, from the admin team to you. There's no way to reply or send a message from your account.

---

## Ratings & History

**Do I have to rate my pickup?**
You'll be prompted to rate a pickup once it's marked "Collected," but this helps us and future pickups run more smoothly.

**Where can I see my past pickups?**
Your dashboard keeps a history of past pickups, so you can look back anytime.

---

## For Admins

**How do I access the Admin Dashboard?**
Click "Admin Login" on the homepage and enter your admin credentials.

**What can I do from the Admin Dashboard?**
View all registered users, view all scheduled pickups with requester details, and send a direct message to any user (delivered both in-app and by email).

**Is admin access separate from regular user accounts?**
Yes — admin login uses separate credentials and is authenticated independently from regular user accounts.

## Troubleshooting

**The site says "Cannot connect to server" — what does that mean?**
This usually means a temporary connectivity issue between your browser and WasteWise's servers. Refresh the page and try again in a moment.

**The page seems slow to load the first time.**
If the app hasn't been used in a little while, the server may need a few seconds to "wake up" on first request — this is normal and resolves after the first load.

**Nothing happens when I click a button.**
Try refreshing the page. If the issue continues, check your internet connection and try again shortly after.
