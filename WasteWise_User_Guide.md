# WasteWise User Guide
### Waste pickup that actually shows up.

Welcome to WasteWise! This guide walks you through everything you can do in the app, step by step.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Creating Your Account

1. Go to the WasteWise homepage.
2. Click **"Create your account"**.
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Full name**
   - **Pickup address** — the address where waste should be collected
   - **Email address**
   - **Password**
4. Click **"Create account"**.

That's it — you're in! You'll be taken straight to your dashboard.

### 2. Logging In

Already have an account?

1. Click **"Log in"** on the homepage.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **"Log in"**.

---

## Using Your Dashboard

Once logged in, your dashboard is your home base. From here you can:
- Sort and schedule waste pickups
- Make payments
- Track an active pickup in real time
- View messages from the WasteWise team
- See your pickup history

---

## Scheduling a Pickup

1. From your dashboard, click **"Schedule pickup"**.
2. **Choose your pickup date** from the available options.
3. **Choose a time window** (e.g. 8:00–10:00 AM).
4. Click **"Confirm pickup"**.

Your pickup is scheduled immediately using the address on your account. You'll receive a **request ID** (e.g. `WW-4821`) you can use to reference this pickup.

*Note: pickup requests currently use the waste category most recently selected on your dashboard. If you'd like to change your pickup address, update it from your Profile.*

### Sorting Your Waste

From your dashboard, select a waste category (Organic, Plastic, Paper, Glass, Metal, or Hazardous) — this is separate from scheduling and helps the collection team know what to expect.

---

## Paying for Your Service

1. From your dashboard, click **"Pay & billing"**.
2. Review the amount due.
3. Click **"Pay"** to complete your payment securely through Paystack.

Payment is handled independently from scheduling a pickup.

---

## Tracking Your Pickup

After scheduling, your dashboard shows a **live status tracker** for your pickup:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | Your request has been received and is confirmed |
| **En Route** | The collection team is on the way |
| **Arrived** | The team has arrived at your address |
| **Collected** | Your waste has been picked up |

No need to refresh constantly — just check back on your dashboard to see where things stand.

---

## After Your Pickup

Once your pickup is marked **Collected**:

1. You'll be prompted to **rate the service**.
2. Your pickup is automatically added to your **history**, so you can look back at past collections anytime.

---

## Messages from the WasteWise Team

If the WasteWise admin team sends you a message (for example, about a schedule change or a service update):

- It will appear directly on your **dashboard**, under Messages.
- You'll also receive a copy by **email**, so you don't have to be logged in to find out.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a card to sign up?**
No — creating an account and scheduling your first pickup doesn't require any payment upfront until you actually confirm a pickup.

**What if I need to change my pickup details after scheduling?**
There's currently no way to edit a pickup once it's scheduled — this is a known limitation we're looking to address in a future update.

**I didn't get an email — what do I do?**
Check your spam/junk folder first. If it's still missing, make sure the email address on your account is correct, and try again — email delivery can occasionally take a few minutes.

**Can I schedule more than one pickup at a time?**
Yes — each pickup gets its own unique request ID, so you can track multiple requests independently.

## For the WasteWise Admin Team

If you manage the WasteWise system, you have access to an additional **Admin Dashboard**:

1. Click **"Admin Login"** on the homepage.
2. Enter your admin credentials.

From the Admin Dashboard you can:
- **View all registered users** and their contact details
- **View all scheduled pickups**, including who requested them and their current status
- **Send a message to any user** — it will appear on their dashboard and be emailed to them directly

---

## Need Help?

WasteWise doesn't yet have a built-in way to contact the team directly from the app — this is on our roadmap. In the meantime, admin messages sent to you will always appear on your dashboard and in your email.
