# WasteWise Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

WasteWise ("we," "us," or "our") provides a platform for scheduling and tracking waste collection pickups. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using WasteWise, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number (optional)
- Pickup/collection address
- Password (stored in encrypted, hashed form — we never store or have access to your plain-text password)
- Waste category selections and pickup scheduling details (date, time window)
- Messages received from the WasteWise admin team

**Payment information:**
Payments are processed through a third-party payment provider (Paystack). WasteWise does not collect, store, or have access to your full card details. We retain only a record that a payment was made, its amount, and its status.

**Automatically collected information:**
We do not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Standard technical information (such as browser type or IP address) may be logged temporarily by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes, in line with their own practices.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Schedule, assign, and track waste pickups
- Process payments for pickup services
- Send you service-related communications (e.g., pickup status updates, messages from our admin team)
- Maintain your pickup history for your reference
- Improve and maintain the reliability of our service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising purposes.

---

## 3. Who We Share Information With

We share information only as necessary to operate WasteWise:

- **Payment processing:** Paystack, to process your pickup payments securely.
- **Email delivery:** A third-party email service provider, to deliver account and pickup-related notifications to your inbox.
- **Hosting & database provider:** Render, which hosts our application and stores account and pickup data on our behalf, under their own security practices.
- **WasteWise admin team:** Authorized administrators can view registered user details and pickup records to operate the service (assign collectors, respond to messages, resolve issues).

We do not share your personal information with any other third party except where required by law.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored in a secured, cloud-hosted database. Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your account and pickup information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the service. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal information from active systems, except where retention is required for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes (such as resolving disputes over past payments).

---

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You may:
- **Access** the personal information associated with your account
- **Update** your account details (such as address or contact information) directly through your dashboard
- **Request deletion** of your account and associated personal data, as this functionality becomes available
- **Opt out** of non-essential communications, where applicable (service-related messages about your own scheduled pickups are necessary to operate the service and cannot be opted out of while your account remains active)

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

WasteWise is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, we will take steps to delete it.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made. Continued use of WasteWise after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

---

## 9. Contact Us

WasteWise does not currently provide a direct contact channel within the app for privacy-related questions or requests. This section will be updated as contact options become available.
